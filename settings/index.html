Hides your apps, swims through the taskbar, naps on the clock… Move the mouse near it and it scurries off.

Pet it by rubbing the mouse over it. Fling it to throw it. Drop a file on it to feed it: the file’s path is copied and Claude opens, so you can paste it in.
